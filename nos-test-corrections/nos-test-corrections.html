02 / Before you begin

Use your feedback carefully.

You need:

  • Your returned paper test
  • This website open on your Chromebook
  • Your science notes and class resources
  • Enough time to think carefully

Do not guess your way through corrections. Use your test, your notes, class materials, and what you have learned.

A check mark means the MC answer was correct. Questions without check marks need correction. Add only those questions.

Part of test correction is learning how to use feedback independently. If you are not sure which question a check mark belongs to:

  1. Look carefully at where the check mark is located.
  2. Compare it with your answer choices.
  3. Determine which question needs correction.
  4. Make a reasonable attempt before asking for help.

Your teacher can clarify directions if you are confused, but will not tell you the correct answer or complete the correction for you. The goal is to identify the misunderstanding and fix it yourself.

 07 / The last step happens in Classroom
Now submit it in Google Classroom.

 
DOWNLOADING THE FILE IS NOT THE SAME AS TURNING IN THE ASSIGNMENT.

 
  1. Download your corrections.
    Click DOWNLOAD MY CORRECTIONS above. Check that the filename contains YOUR first name, last name, and period. Example: Smith_Jane_Period3_NOS_Corrections.txt. If a save box opens, choose Downloads and click Save.
  2. Return to Google Classroom.
    Open the Nature of Science — Test Corrections & Short-Answer Revisions assignment. Choose View instructions if needed.
  3. Attach your file.
    Under Your work, choose Add or create → File. Choose Upload or Browse if shown. Open Downloads, select the .txt file you just downloaded, and click Open. Click Upload or Add if asked.
  4. Check the name.
    The attached filename must contain YOUR name and period. Do not submit a file with another student’s name. Choose the newest final file, not an older draft.
  5. Check the attachment.
    Wait until the file appears under Your work. Open it to check your name and answers. If the wrong file is attached, remove that attachment and add the correct file.
  6. Turn it in.
    Click Turn in. Confirm if Google Classroom asks again.
  7. Verify.
    Make sure the assignment says Turned in. If it does not, or an error appears, ask your teacher for help.
