ASSIGNMENT INSTRUCTIONS
Today you are looking for science connections in things you already care about. You are not choosing your final project yet. There are no “correct” interests.

1. Open the Project Discovery website: [PASTE YOUR GITHUB PAGES URL HERE]
2. Choose your grade. Enter the Class-Only ID your teacher gave you and your class period. Check the code carefully. Do not type your name anywhere in the questionnaire.
3. Answer thoughtfully. Most questions use checkboxes or ratings. A phrase or one or two sentences is enough for written answers. “I don’t know yet” is okay. Leave names, account names, contact information, and private personal details out of your answers.

5. At Review, check your ID and your answers. Finish any required items that are listed. Check both review boxes.

8. Check that the attached filename shows your correct grade and Class-Only ID. Click Turn in, confirm, and check for Turned in.
9. Once you have checked your downloaded file, use the website’s Clear saved copy button, especially on a shared Chromebook.

IF YOU DO NOT FINISH TODAY
